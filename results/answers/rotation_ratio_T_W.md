The rotation ratio T/|W| is defined as the ratio of rotational kinetic energy T to gravitational binding energy |W| of the inner core at bounce (Richers et al., 2017). This parameter determines the centrifugal deformation of the inner core and thus the mass quadrupole moment that drives gravitational wave (GW) emission (Akhmetali et al., 2026).

Quantities that depend on the rotation ratio T/|W| include:

1. The bounce signal amplitude Δh_+ (Richers et al., 2017): For T/|W| ≤ 0.06, the amplitude increases linearly with rotation rate, with a similar slope for all equations of state (EOS). At higher rotation rates, the curves diverge from the linear relationship due to centrifugal support.

2. The maximum GW strain produced at bounce (Abdikamalov et al., 2014; Abdikamalov Review, 2022): The GW strain h increases linearly with T/|W| for T/|W| ≤ 0.06–0.09, but the growth slows for larger T/|W|, and then h starts decreasing with T/|W| for T/|W| ≥ 0.17.

3. The peak frequency f_peak (Afle et al., 2021): The postbounce oscillations depend on the equation of state, which manifests itself through the characteristic frequency of the oscillations, f_peak.

4. The bounce amplitude DΔh (Akhmetali et al., 2026): For T/|W| ≤ 0.06, the amplitude scales approximately as DΔh ∝ T/|W|. At higher rotation rates, centrifugal support slows the collapse and bounce dynamics, offsetting the increased deformation and causing the GW amplitude to saturate.

5. The dynamics of the core bounce and ring-down oscillations (Abdikamalov Review, 2022): The GW signature of these phenomena is mostly determined by the mass, radius, and the rotation parameter of the inner core. For T/|W| ≤ 0.06–0.09, the GW signal is not sensitive to the degree of differential rotation A, while for higher T/|W|, the GW signal depends on A.

The dependence of these quantities on T/|W| reflects the dynamical importance of rotation in core-collapse supernovae, with linear scaling for low rotation rates and saturation effects at higher rotation rates due to centrifugal support.
